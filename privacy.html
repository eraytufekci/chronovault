ChronoVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Short version: ChronoVault processes 100% of your browsing history and bookmarks locally inside your browser. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your browsing data, bookmarks, or personal information.

1. Data Accessed

ChronoVault requests Chrome's history, bookmarks, downloads, and storage permissions exclusively to provide its core functionality when you open the extension popup:

  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Read locally on your device only when you filter, scan for duplicates, restore a backup file, or export to CSV, Excel (.xlsx), JSON, or HTML. None of this data is ever sent to any external server.
  • Local Storage: Used solely to remember your optional Pro license activation state inside your browser (chrome.storage.local).

2. Optional Pro License Verification

If you purchase ChronoVault Pro and manually enter a license key, the extension sends only the license key and our public Polar.sh organization identifier to Polar's public license validation API (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/validate) to confirm whether the key is active. No browsing history, bookmarks, or usage analytics are included in that request.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

ChronoVault contains no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party analytics scripts. Purchases of ChronoVault Pro are processed securely by Polar.sh as Merchant of Record under Polar's own privacy policy.

4. Contact

If you have any privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
